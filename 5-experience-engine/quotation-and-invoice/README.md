# Quotation and Invoice

This folder now contains structured invoice, receipt, document-link, and bank
transfer output definitions extracted from JVTO's working Blade templates and
customer portal.

Available data:

- `invoice-and-receipt-templates.json`

Current coverage:

- reservation invoice email structure;
- payment receipt structure;
- invoice, receipt, and add-on invoice portal links;
- bank transfer upload/payment account page definition.

Not stored here:

- real customer invoice instances;
- runtime customer names, emails, phones, booking URLs, or payment links.

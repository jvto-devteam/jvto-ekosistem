# Email Templates

This folder now contains structured transactional email/template definitions
from JVTO's current reservation invoice and receipt attachment views.

Available data:

- `transactional-email-templates.json`

Current coverage:

- reservation invoice email;
- payment receipt attachment/rendered receipt structure.

Not stored here:

- actual sent email logs;
- real customer names, emails, phone numbers, booking URLs, or payment links.

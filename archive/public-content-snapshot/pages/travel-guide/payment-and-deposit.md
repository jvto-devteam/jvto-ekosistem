## Payment & Deposit

A deposit secures the booking, with the balance due before the tour; exact amounts and deadlines are on the booking page.

A deposit (standard 20% of the booking value) secures a booking, with the balance due before the tour starts; close-to-departure bookings may require fuller payment. Exact amounts, accepted methods, and deadlines are shown at checkout and on the booking information page.

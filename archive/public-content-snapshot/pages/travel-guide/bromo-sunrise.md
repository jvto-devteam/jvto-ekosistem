• early jeep pickup

## Mount Bromo

Active East Java volcano in the Tengger caldera "Sea of Sand", famous for its pre-dawn sunrise panorama reached by 4WD jeep.

Typical schedule: Pre-dawn departure (around 03:00) by 4WD jeep for the sunrise viewpoint, then a crossing of the sand sea and a short ascent to the crater rim; finish mid-morning.

You need: TNBTS (Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park) ticket, 4WD jeep for the sand sea.
